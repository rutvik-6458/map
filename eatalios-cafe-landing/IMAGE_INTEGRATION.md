# Image Integration Guide

This document outlines where to add images for the Eatalio's Cafe landing page.

## Gallery Images

Add the following images to `public/gallery/`:

1. **pasta-1.jpg** - Pasta close-up shot
   - High-quality photo of pasta dish
   - Recommended: 800x800px or larger
   - Format: JPG, WebP, or AVIF

2. **pizza-1.jpg** - Exotica Pizza
   - Photo of the signature Exotica Pizza
   - Recommended: 800x800px or larger
   - Format: JPG, WebP, or AVIF

3. **interior-1.jpg** - Cafe interior and seating
   - Photo showing the calm and pleasant ambience
   - Recommended: 1200x800px or larger
   - Format: JPG, WebP, or AVIF

4. **food-spread-1.jpg** - Food and beverage spread
   - Photo showing multiple dishes and beverages
   - Recommended: 1200x800px or larger
   - Format: JPG, WebP, or AVIF

5. **exterior-1.jpg** - Exterior view at Prime Shoppers
   - Photo of the cafe exterior
   - Recommended: 1200x800px or larger
   - Format: JPG, WebP, or AVIF

6. **pasta-2.jpg** - Additional pasta dish
   - Another pasta photo for variety
   - Recommended: 800x800px or larger
   - Format: JPG, WebP, or AVIF

## How to Update

1. Add images to `public/gallery/` folder
2. Open `src/components/Gallery.tsx`
3. Uncomment the Image component code (lines with `// Uncomment when images are available:`)
4. Remove or comment out the placeholder div

## Image Optimization Tips

- Use Next.js Image component for automatic optimization
- Compress images before adding (use tools like TinyPNG, Squoosh)
- Use WebP or AVIF format for better performance
- Maintain aspect ratios for consistent gallery layout

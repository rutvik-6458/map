# Image Requirements for Kenny's Kitchen Landing Page

## Required Images

Place the following images in the `public/images/` directory:

### 1. Hero Image
- **File**: `public/images/hero.jpg`
- **Description**: Hero section background image
- **Recommended**: Exterior view of Kenny's Kitchen on VIP Road, or cafe interior showing the relaxed ambience
- **Aspect Ratio**: 16:9 or wider
- **Size**: High resolution (1920x1080 or larger)

### 2. Gallery Images

#### Gallery Image 1
- **File**: `public/images/gallery1.jpg`
- **Description**: Pavbhaji Pie close-up
- **Recommended**: Professional food photography of the signature Pavbhaji Pie dish

#### Gallery Image 2
- **File**: `public/images/gallery2.jpg`
- **Description**: Cafe interior and seating
- **Recommended**: Interior shot showing the relaxed ambience and comfortable seating

#### Gallery Image 3
- **File**: `gallery3.jpg`
- **Description**: Coffee and beverages
- **Recommended**: Irish Coffee or other beverages served at the cafe

#### Gallery Image 4
- **File**: `public/images/gallery4.jpg`
- **Description**: Burgers and fries
- **Recommended**: Peri Peri Fries or other popular menu items

#### Gallery Image 5
- **File**: `public/images/gallery5.jpg`
- **Description**: Exterior view on VIP Road
- **Recommended**: Exterior shot of the cafe showing the location on VIP Road

## Image Specifications

- **Format**: JPG or WebP (JPG recommended for compatibility)
- **Quality**: High quality, optimized for web
- **Aspect Ratio**: 4:3 for gallery images (will be cropped to fit)
- **File Size**: Optimize images to keep file sizes reasonable (under 500KB each for gallery, under 1MB for hero)

## Temporary Placeholders

Until actual images are available, you can:
1. Use placeholder services like `https://via.placeholder.com/1920x1080` for hero
2. Use food stock photos temporarily
3. Create simple colored placeholder images

## Notes

- All images should be optimized for web performance
- Consider using Next.js Image component's built-in optimization
- Ensure images are properly licensed for commercial use
- Images should reflect the actual cafe experience and food quality
